# Content Review Rules

For repositories that publish reader-facing content.

- Production pages must never show test, demo, placeholder, "under
  construction", "coming soon", draft, preview, or internal editorial phrasing.
- Draft and preview content must not appear in the homepage, category pages,
  sitemap, RSS, or search.
- Do not publish an item unless the user explicitly approves that item.
- Keep URLs and slugs stable unless a redirect plan is approved.
- Strip internal review notes from frontmatter before publication.
- Prefer reader-facing language over internal strategy language: the closing
  section should speak to a reader, not to an editor.
- Check for template-like sections that repeat across items. They read as
  machine-generated and are the first thing a reviewer notices.
